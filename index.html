Shanmukh’s Gym: Membership Management System

A web app that a gym front desk can run: add members, sell membership plans, track renewals and see revenue on a dashboard.

Role-based security

Admin has full access. Staff cannot change plans or delete members. Rules are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the menu.

Safe logins

BCrypt-hashed passwords, CSRF protection on every form, and secrets kept out of the repository.

Real business rules

No overlapping memberships, the price is saved at purchase time, and status is calculated from dates. All run in transactional service methods.

Efficient queries

JPQL aggregates for the dashboard, a NOT EXISTS subquery for members who have not renewed, and fetch joins to avoid N+1 queries.
